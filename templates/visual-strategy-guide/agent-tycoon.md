# Agent Tycoon — Strategy Guide

## Objective

Reach **$10M valuation** before running out of funds.

## Phase 1: Garage Startup (Days 1-30)

### Priority Actions

1. **Hire your first agent**
   - Start with **Coder** ($500/month)
   - Coder earns $800-1,200/month via coding tasks
   - Net: +$300-700/month

2. **Take low-risk tasks**
   - Simple content writing ($50-100)
   - Basic research ($75-150)
   - Avoid DeFi/complex coding early (risk of failure)

3. **Train Coder skills**
   - Invest $200 in coding training
   - Increases earnings by 20%
   - ROI: 2-3 months

### Budget Management

| Category | Budget | % of Total |
|----------|--------|------------|
| Agent salaries | $500 | 50% |
| Training | $200 | 20% |
| Tasks (failure risk) | $200 | 20% |
| Savings | $100 | 10% |

**Target:** End Phase 1 with $2,000-3,000 in savings.

## Phase 2: Scaling (Days 31-90)

### Hire Second Agent

**Trader** ($600/month)
- Earns $1,000-1,500/month via DeFi tasks
- ROI: +$400-900/month
- Requires Coder at skill level 2+ for infrastructure

### Task Portfolio

| Task Type | Risk | Reward | Recommended |
|-----------|------|--------|-------------|
| Content | Low | $50-100 | 40% |
| Research | Low-Medium | $75-150 | 30% |
| Coding | Medium | $200-400 | 20% |
| DeFi | High | $500-1,000 | 10% |

### Upgrade Skills

- **Coder → Level 3** ($500): +30% earnings
- **Trader → Level 2** ($400): +20% earnings

**Target:** End Phase 2 with $15,000-20,000 in savings.

## Phase 3: Diversification (Days 91-180)

### Hire Third Agent

**Writer** ($450/month)
- Earns $700-1,000/month via content tasks
- Boosts social media presence (+10% task success rate)

### Hire Fourth Agent

**Analyst** ($700/month)
- Earns $1,100-1,600/month via research tasks
- Unlocks premium DeFi opportunities (2x reward, 1.5x risk)

### Strategy Shift

- **DeFi tasks:** 10% → 25% of portfolio
- **Content + Research:** 60% → 55% of portfolio
- **Coding:** 20% → 15% of portfolio

**Target:** End Phase 3 with $100,000-150,000 in savings.

## Phase 4: Unicorn Push (Days 181-365)

### Hire Remaining Specialists

| Agent | Cost | Earnings | ROI |
|-------|------|----------|-----|
| **Auditor** | $600 | $900-1,200 | +$300-600 |
| **Consultant** | $550 | $800-1,100 | +$250-550 |
| **Marketer** | $500 | $700-950 | +$200-450 |

### Portfolio Optimization

- **DeFi:** 25% → 35% (high reward, manageable risk with 8 agents)
- **Content:** 30% → 25% (focus on high-impact posts)
- **Research:** 25% → 25% (maintain steady income)
- **Coding:** 15% → 15% (essential for infrastructure)

### Roguelite Events

| Event | Impact | Mitigation |
|-------|--------|------------|
| Market crash | -30% DeFi earnings | Diversify into content/research |
| Tech breakthrough | +50% coding demand | Level up Coder |
| Regulation | -20% overall | Maintain cash reserve |
| Viral content | +100% content earnings | Double down on Writer |

**Target:** Reach $10M valuation by Day 365.

## Pro Tips

1. **Never spend all your cash.** Keep 20% as reserve for bad runs.
2. **Level up before hiring.** Agent synergy matters.
3. **Watch for events.** Roguelite events change optimal strategy.
4. **Pay via x402** (easter egg): Unlock bonus multiplier on successful DeFi tasks.
5. **Restart if hopeless.** It's a roguelite — permadeath is part of the game.

## Loss Conditions

- **Bankruptcy:** Funds ≤ $0
- **Failed scaling:** Can't pay salaries for 3 consecutive months
- **Talent drain:** 2+ agents quit (happens if you don't pay bonuses)

## Win Condition

- **Unicorn status:** Valuation ≥ $10M
- **Sustainable:** 12 months of positive cash flow
- **Happy team:** All agents at skill level 3+
