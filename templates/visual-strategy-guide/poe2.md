# Path of Exile 2 — Strategy Guide

## Patch 0.5.2 Status

- **Warrior class:** Viable, buffed in 0.5.2
- **Key changes:** Improved survivability, damage scaling
- **Meta:** Tanky builds favored, glass cannon nerfed

## Phase 1: Early Game (Act 1-3)

### Build Archetypes

| Archetype | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|-----------|------------|
| **Tank Warrior** | High survivability, sustainable | Slower clear speed |
| **Frost Witch** | Great crowd control, high damage | Squishy, needs positioning |
| **Ranger** | Fast clear, good mobility | Vulnerable to burst damage |

### Recommended: Tank Warrior

**Skill priorities:**
1. Charge Dash (mobility)
2. Ground Slam (AoE damage)
3. Fortify (survivability)

**Passive tree focus:**
- Life nodes first (100+ life by Act 3)
- Resists (75% elemental resist minimum)
- Block chance (chance to block attacks)

### Gear Checklist

| Slot | Priority | Stats to Look For |
|------|----------|-------------------|
| Weapon | 1 | Base damage, attack speed |
| Chest | 2 | Life, armor |
| Helmet | 2 | Life, resists |
| Boots | 3 | Movement speed, life |
| Gloves | 3 | Life, attack speed |

**Tip:** Don't overspend on early-game gear. Save for uniques later.

## Phase 2: Mid Game (Act 4-6)

### Skill Upgrades

- **Ground Slam → Earthquake** (more AoE)
- **Charge Dash → Leap Slam** (better mobility)
- **Add:** War Cry (buff), Molten Shell (armor)

### Passive Tree Re-Spec

- Shift focus from pure life → damage nodes
- Add melee physical damage
- Add critical strike chance (10-15%)

### Key Uniques

| Unique | Slot | Effect |
|--------|------|--------|
| **Tabula Rasa** | Chest | 6-linked white sockets |
| **Geofri's Baptism** | Weapon | +100% physical damage |
| **The Anvil** | Amulet | +30% block chance |

## Phase 3: Endgame (Maps, Bosses)

### Map Strategy

- **Yellow maps:** Focus on clearing, ignore most bosses
- **Red maps:** Target specific boss loot tables
- **Boss priority:** MapBoss → UberBoss → Shaper/Elder (if accessible)

### Skill Finalization

- **Main skill:** Cyclone + Multistrike + Melee Phys + Impale
- **Mobility:** Flame Dash
- **Utility:** Vaal Haste (speed), Vaal Discipline (ES recovery)

### Passive Tree Final

- Target: 200+ life, 75% all resists, 40+ block chance
- Add: Crit damage (150%+), attack speed (80%+)
- Cluster jewels for power spikes

## Advanced Strategies

### Corpse Management

- **Ground Slam** creates corpses for explosions
- **Volatile Dead** consumes corpses for massive damage
- **Proximity Shield** positioning matters

### Boss Mechanics

| Boss | Key Mechanic | Counter |
|------|--------------|---------|
| **MapBoss** | Teleport → burst | Keep distance, Flame Dash out |
| **UberBoss** | Phase transition → AoE | Time cooldowns, save mobility skills |
| **Shaper** | Bullet hell | Circle strafe, use Flame Dash |

### Loot Filtering

- **Keep:** Chaos orbs, regal orbs, vaal orbs
- **Vendor trash:** White items, 1-link gear
- **Sell:** Rare items with good stats (life, resists, damage)

## Patch 0.5.2 Specifics

- **Warrior buffed:** +15% base damage, +10% life regen
- **Witch nerfed:** Frostbolt cast time increased
- **Ranger unchanged:** Still strong for speed clears

## Hardcore Tips

1. **Play safe, not fast.** One death = restart.
2. **Prioritize resists** over damage in Acts 1-3.
3. **Save for key uniques** (Tabula Rasa, The Anvil).
4. **Build sustain first** (life regen, leech), then damage.
5. **Check patch notes** every update — meta shifts fast.

## Current Meta (June 2026)

**Top tier:** Tank Warrior, Frost Witch (slightly nerfed), Ranger

**Mid tier:** Miner, Cyclone Berserker, Totems

**Low tier:** Pure glass cannon (buffed but still risky in HC)
